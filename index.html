How it works

Set it once. We do the refreshing, restock-checking, and code-hunting for you.

01

Pick your stores & sizes

Choose the budget stores you actually shop and set your top size and jeans waist/length. Two minutes, once.

02

We watch everything

We keep an eye on prices, restocks, sale events, and discount codes across those stores — including items that sold out in your size.

03

You get a buyable alert

When something drops or restocks in your size, you get one alert with the price, the code if there is one, and a direct link to buy it.

Why charge at all?

Watching stores around the clock, checking sizes and codes, and sending timely alerts costs money to run. A small flat subscription keeps the service independent — no selling your data, no pushing sponsored "deals" that aren't actually deals.

If the numbers don't work at ~$5/month, we'd rather not launch than launch something that spams you.

Questions

What counts as a "deal" worth alerting me about?

A meaningful price drop on an item, a restock of something that was sold out in your size, a new sale event at a store you follow, or a working discount code — especially one that stacks on sale prices. Tiny 5% wiggles and fake "was" prices don't trigger alerts.

How often will alerts arrive?

Only when there's a match in your sizes at your stores — for most people that's a handful a week, more during big sale periods. You'll be able to set a daily digest instead of instant alerts, and a maximum frequency, so your inbox never gets flooded.

How do I change my sizes or stores?

Every alert email includes a link to update your sizes and store list — no account password needed. Change them whenever your fit or taste changes and the very next alerts use the new settings.

How do I unsubscribe?

One tap, in every email. No retention flow, no "are you sure" maze. Unsubscribing deletes your alert preferences along with stopping the emails.

Is this a live service? When does it launch?

Honestly: not yet. DripDrop (working name) is in validation right now. This waitlist is how we find out whether enough budget streetwear shoppers actually want this. If enough people join, we build and launch it — waitlist members first. If not, it won't launch, and we'll email you once to say so and delete your details. Joining is free and creates no commitment either way.
